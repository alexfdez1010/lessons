A debt-funded repurchase can raise EPS while widening equity downside. A tax deduction can have value without making every loan desirable. This course builds the dated budgets and state-by-state comparisons needed to distinguish an operating gain, a financing transfer and a real cost.

This advanced course assumes operating financial statements and DCF, portfolio risk and bond cash flows. It studies non-financial companies, with explicit invented contracts and tax rules. The banking course treats bank-specific balance sheets separately. Complete all five chapters before the direct final exam.
