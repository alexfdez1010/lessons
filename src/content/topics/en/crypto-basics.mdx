You open a crypto wallet and see a balance. The wallet has not put digital coins inside your phone: it uses keys to authorise changes to a shared record. Understanding that distinction connects wallets, signatures and the blockchain that records transactions.

The course introduces those parts without assuming previous crypto knowledge, then follows a transaction from signing to confirmation and compares two ways of recording ownership.

- **The chain itself** — blocks, hashes, and how hashes reveal altered records and consensus makes accepted history costly to replace.
- **Keys and wallets** — the public/private key pair that authorises transactions, and the seed phrase that backs it up.
- **Wallets without storage** — how a "wallet" can hold money it never actually stores.
- **A transaction's journey** — from your screen to the mempool to a confirmed block and why inclusion and confirmation times vary.
- **The fee** — how it influences inclusion without guaranteeing a place in the next block.
- **UTXO vs. account** — Bitcoin's model of digital coins versus Ethereum's model of running balances.
