A private fund can have a large commitment, a smaller amount paid in and a different residual asset value. This course follows those separate ledgers through a specified closed-end partnership model. It applies the valuation and performance concepts from the prerequisites to the actual LP/GP contract, rather than treating all fund structures or fee quotes as identical.

All numerical agreements and market paths are invented, in a stated currency and with dated cash flows. Legal rights, consent and liability depend on the relevant documents and jurisdiction.

- **Structure and commitments** — locate the fund, LP, GP and manager; reconcile what has been promised, paid, invested and expensed.
- **Calls and liquidity** — preserve settlement dates, track explicit recall/recycling terms and test the next call before counting later proceeds.
- **Fees and carry** — calculate bases and offsets, preferred distributions, catch-up and the gap between clawback owed and collected.
- **Performance and benchmarks** — distinguish distributed cash from residual NAV, apply interim IRR and match a declared public-index cash account.
- **Valuation and financing** — bridge portfolio equity to fund NAV, reconcile secondary prices and transferred obligations, and separate subscription-line timing from wealth.

The final exam applies all five lessons. The course gives no product recommendation, universal LPA terms or promised private-market return.
