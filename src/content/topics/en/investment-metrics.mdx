Two funds both gained 20% over five years. One fell 40% along the way; the other never fell more than 8%. Their ending returns match, but the paths expose you to different risks. A useful comparison needs the return, the time taken and the losses endured.

These lessons turn those questions into measures and explain what each measure leaves out:

- **Return (ROI & CAGR)** — how much you made, and how fast it actually compounded.
- **Volatility** — the dispersion of returns, which measures variability rather than every form of risk.
- **Max drawdown** — the worst peak-to-trough fall, the metric that tests your stomach.
- **Sharpe & Sortino** — returns relative to total variability or downside deviation, using consistent periods and benchmarks.
- **Alpha & beta** — return relative to a chosen model and sensitivity to its benchmark; neither alone proves skill.
