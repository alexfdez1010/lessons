A bank’s own figures do not fully describe the consequences of its failure. You also need to know who lent to it and how those institutions are connected. A **graph** records entities as nodes and relationships as edges; a **graph neural network (GNN)** uses those links to combine information across neighbours.

Building on deep learning, the course develops message passing and architecture choices for financial networks. Applications to contagion, fraud and relational signals are paired with evaluation that respects time, topology and the information available at prediction.

- **Markets are graphs, not tables** — the representation shift: nodes and edges, directed vs undirected, weighted vs binary, the node/edge/graph feature stack, and the four canonical finance graphs (interbank lending, supply chains, asset-correlation networks, on-chain wallet/transaction graphs).
- **Message passing and the three tasks** — aggregate-from-neighbours as the core of the message-passing architectures studied here, why permutation-invariant pooling is non-negotiable, how stacking layers grows the receptive field hop by hop, and the three task types: **node-level** (classify/score an entity), **edge-level** (predict a link), **graph-level** (score a whole network state).
- **GCN, GraphSAGE, GAT and temporal GNNs** — the architecture lineage: spectral-flavoured **GCN** (fixed degree-normalised averaging), inductive **GraphSAGE** (sample-and-aggregate that generalises to unseen nodes), **GAT** (learned attention over neighbours), and **temporal/dynamic GNNs** for a network whose topology changes every day.
- **Systemic risk and contagion** — the killer application: the **default cascade** through an interbank network, **DebtRank** and the feedback loops omitted by a per-firm model without relational features, and how a GNN learns a fragility score that *embeds* the topology.
- **Fraud rings and relational alpha** — **AML ring detection** on transaction graphs (the signal is the structure, not any one account), **relational/peer-firm alpha** propagating a signal across supply-chain and co-movement edges, and **on-chain analytics** on wallet graphs.
- **The honest audit** — why graphs leak so badly (a node sees its neighbours, so a careless split lets the test set peek across edges), **topology-aware splitting**, **over-smoothing** as you stack too many layers, **non-stationary topology**, and the brutal data-plumbing cost that sinks most GNN projects before they predict anything.
