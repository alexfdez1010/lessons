Insurance exchanges a premium for a specified promise when an uncertain event occurs. The useful comparison follows retained losses, eligibility, limits and payment dates—not just the price on the quote. This course builds on cash accounting, time value and basic financial risk. All numerical contracts and probabilities are invented; real terms and legal protections depend on product and jurisdiction.

You will learn to:

- Identify policyholder, insured, insurer and beneficiary, and reconcile premium with retained loss.
- Calculate explicitly ordered deductibles, percentage shares, payment caps and aggregate limits.
- Derive how independent pooling stabilises an average, then construct the common risk it cannot remove.
- Separate selection, incentives and claim evidence from automatic conclusions about behaviour or eligibility.
- Reconcile claim estimates, primary obligations, reinsurance collections and cash due today.

The final exam tests all five lessons. Life-annuity design and retirement planning belong to their separate course.
