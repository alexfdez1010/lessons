A customer deposit, a bank reserve balance and shareholder equity are different claims. Reading both sides of each entry reveals how bank lending, payments and central-bank transactions connect without treating every reserve increase as a profit or a fixed lending multiplier.

This intermediate course builds on money accounts, economic policy, loans and bonds. It starts with named counterparties, then adds dated funding needs, annual income and conditional stress tests. All quantitative examples are invented and state the scope of their rules.
