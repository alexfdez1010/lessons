A large account and a promised annual payment are different resources. A positive present-value scenario can still leave a bill unpaid today, while a flexible withdrawal rule can stay affordable by reducing consumption. This course puts those distinctions on a dated household cash ledger.

All numerical contracts, tax rules and mortality laws are fictional and explicitly stated. The course teaches scenario accounting and model limits; it establishes no universal safe withdrawal percentage, personal recommendation or country-specific entitlement.
