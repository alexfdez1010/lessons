A vault sells options and distributes premiums. To understand its return, follow what happens when the underlying rallies beyond a covered call’s strike or falls through a cash-secured put’s strike. The premium compensates for a changed payoff; it does not remove losses.

Using Volatility Trading and DeFi Derivatives & Perpetuals, this course studies that exposure inside smart contracts, then adds oracle freshness, settlement, gas and liquidity constraints. Funding and basis are examined as positioning information, rather than as direct measurements of future volatility.

- **On-chain options protocols** — how an option becomes a smart contract: full collateralization vs the capital-inefficiency it forces, AMM-quoted vol vs on-chain order books, European cash-settled vs physically-settled designs, and why pricing an option on-chain is so much harder than swapping two tokens.
- **DeFi option vaults (DOVs)** — the structured-product boom: covered-call and cash-secured-put vaults that auto-roll every epoch, sell options through a weekly auction, and hand depositors a headline "yield." What that yield actually is, who buys the other side, and the payoff of selling part of the upside for a premium.
- **The structural short-vol risk** — the catch nobody puts in the marketing: a DOV is *systematically short volatility*, harvesting the volatility risk premium with a negatively-skewed payoff. Why the auctions can underprice, why crowding makes it reflexive, and what a crash does to "yield."
- **On-chain implied vol & oracles** — where the volatility number even comes from: on-chain vol surfaces and DVOL-style indices, the problem of a delayed oracle whose value stays fixed between updates, and how a stale vol read becomes an arbitrageur's free lunch.
- **Perp funding as a vol & skew signal** — the perpetual's funding rate and basis as a live broadcast of leverage, sentiment, and the cost of carry — and how desks read funding to infer skew, positioning, and the limits of using it to anticipate a volatility-regime change.
- **Settlement, liquidity & frictions** — the unglamorous reality: gas costs on every roll, oracle-settled expiries and the MEV that swarms them, pin risk, fragmented liquidity, and the full TradFi-vs-DeFi comparison of what on-chain actually costs you.
