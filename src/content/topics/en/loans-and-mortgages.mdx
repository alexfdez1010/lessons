Two loans offer the same monthly payment. One lasts three years; the other lasts six and charges an upfront fee. The instalment alone cannot tell you which costs less: you need the amount borrowed, the payment schedule, the rate and the charges.

Building on interest and effective annual rates, this course follows a loan from the first payment to early repayment or default:

- **Loan anatomy** — principal, interest, the rate, and the term, plus what makes a mortgage special.
- **Amortization schedules** — how early payments can contain a larger interest share, how the split flips over time, and exactly where each month's money goes.
- **Fixed vs variable rates** — which one shifts the risk onto you, and when each makes sense.
- **APR vs APY** — how lenders actually quote rates, so you read the **total cost of credit**, not just the monthly payment.
- **Down payment, loan-to-value & mortgage insurance** — how much you put down, what ratio it leaves, and how insurance requirements vary by loan and jurisdiction.
- **Fees & closing costs** — the charges hiding in the fine print.
- **Life after signing** — early repayment, refinancing, default, and foreclosure.
