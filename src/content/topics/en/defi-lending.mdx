In a collateralized DeFi money market, suppliers place assets in a pool and borrowers lock collateral to borrow from it. Smart contracts calculate interest and enforce borrowing limits. The collateral reduces the lender’s exposure, but a rapid price fall, an oracle error or insufficient liquidation liquidity can still leave losses.

The course connects interest and Ethereum to the borrower’s health factor, the supplier’s receipt token and the protocol’s interest-rate curve.

- **What DeFi lending is** — pooled liquidity instead of a banker, algorithmic rates, and the overcollateralized borrowing model used in the markets studied here.
- **Collateral, loan-to-value & borrowing power** — the borrower's-eye view of how much cash your locked assets can unlock.
- **The health factor** — the single number that measures how close you are to trouble.
- **Liquidation** — how liquidators repay debt against collateral, and why this does not guarantee full recovery.
- **The interest-rate curve** — how utilization gets turned into a price.
- **Receipt tokens** — the aTokens and cTokens that quietly compound in your wallet.
- **Oracle failures, bad debt & protocol risk** — the things that actually break.
