Two swaps use the same liquidity pool. Execute the first one and it changes the price available to the second. Whoever controls their order may capture value from that price change. **Maximal Extractable Value (MEV)** names value available through transaction inclusion, exclusion or ordering beyond ordinary block rewards and fees.

Using Ethereum and automated market makers as the starting point, the course examines how public pending transactions, private orderflow and block construction create different opportunities and protections:

- **What MEV is** — why the right to order transactions has value at all, and how inclusion and sequencing affect execution.
- **Mempool & ordering mechanism** — how transactions are picked, and how priority fees turned ordering into a live auction.
- **Front-running & back-running** — trading immediately before or after another transaction.
- **The sandwich attack** — the signature DeFi predator, worked out with exact constant-product math so you can compute the victim's loss and the attacker's profit yourself.
- **Arbitrage & liquidations** — how price alignment and collateral enforcement can create MEV, alongside competition and execution costs.
- **Countermeasures** — Proposer-Builder Separation, MEV-Boost, relays, private orderflow, fair ordering and encrypted mempools.
