## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Read the payment terms as well as the rate">
Separate principal, interest, fees and new borrowing. Check how each payment changes
the balance and whether it meets the actual agreement's minimum. Compare matched
cost and rate assumptions, keep collateral and default risks in view, and update a
payoff projection when transactions or terms change. Include any balloon and later
recast in the dated cash-flow plan; a fixed rate does not guarantee unchanged
instalments across different repayment phases.
</Callout>
