This exam covers five lessons on prediction-market claims, order books, resolution, forecasting, complete sets and position sizing. Unless stated otherwise, simple arithmetic assumes a fully funded standard binary claim paying one settlement unit or zero, no additional costs and one unit equal to one reporting dollar. For Kelly, the fraction means funded cash at risk, and the probability forecasts the specified contract payout. Current product exceptions, fees, collateral and access need their stated treatment.
