Use only the assumptions given. An expected PV is not an insurance quote or sufficient individual reserve; an unpaid request is not paid cash or automatic borrowing.

## Keep the accounting connected

Date and label each resource, state payment conditions, separate cash paid from shortfall, and compare consumption and ending assets on one basis. A finite model is a tool for inspecting assumptions, not a guarantee of an entire remaining lifetime.
