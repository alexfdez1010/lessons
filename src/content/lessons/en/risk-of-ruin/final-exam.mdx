<Callout variant="tip" title="What to take into a risk calculation">
Define the first-crossing event, stake rule and horizon. Use the exact fixed-unit formula only for its ±1 walk; distinguish a Brownian approximation and a fractional-wealth simulation. Check input uncertainty, practical floors and drawdowns, and keep planned stop losses separate from realised execution.
