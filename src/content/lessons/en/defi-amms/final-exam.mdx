This exam covers the five AMM lessons: pool trading, constant-product mechanics, liquidity claims, execution risks and LP performance. The basic reserve and LP-token questions use the course’s full-range v2-style model. For simple IL questions, assume a fee-free full-range 50/50-by-value position with no liquidity changes, and let r be the final A-in-B price divided by its entry price. All compared portfolios use the same terminal currency and date unless the question states otherwise. Actual fees, costs and concentrated ranges require their stated treatment.

## Course Recap

<Callout variant="success">
Use the stated pool model to explain reserve changes and execution prices. Then value the LP and its holding benchmark on the same date, separating relative underperformance from absolute gains or losses.
</Callout>
