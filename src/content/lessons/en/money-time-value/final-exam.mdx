## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Use the result with its assumptions">
A reproducible valuation names the viewpoint, amounts, dates, period unit and rate. The forward and backward calculations should agree at the same date. Check the certainty and comparability of the promised payments before using the result to make a decision.
</Callout>
