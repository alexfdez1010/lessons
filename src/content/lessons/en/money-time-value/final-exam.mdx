## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Use the result with its assumptions">
A reproducible valuation names the viewpoint, amounts, dates, period unit and rate. The forward and backward calculations should agree at the same date. Check certainty, comparability, financing and other constraints before using the result to make a decision. A positive model NPV is not a funding source or a guarantee.
</Callout>
