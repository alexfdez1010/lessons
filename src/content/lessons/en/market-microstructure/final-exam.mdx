## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Read the market state before the price">
Identify continuous matching or an auction phase, the eligible order set and the priority rules. Compare price acceptance with allocation, a snapshot with a final result, and the chosen benchmark with the costs it measures. Check the venue's cutoffs, protections and unfilled-order rules before treating a quote or indication as an execution promise. Check the unfilled quantity and remainder policy as well as the price of executed shares; apply explicit joint probabilities and keep subsequent inventory performance separate from purchase cost.
</Callout>
