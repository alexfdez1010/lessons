## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="What to keep checking">
Use the statements together: an accounting profit and a cash receipt can occur at different times. Match each ratio to the balances and flows it measures. CCC links inventory, collection and supplier credit, but does not replace a cash forecast. A valuation depends on its assumptions, while accounting warning signs identify questions to investigate rather than proving fraud on their own. For project choices, inspect IRR roots and compare feasible signed cash-flow alternatives at an explicit opportunity-cost rate; a project percentage does not determine later portfolio wealth. Growth needs reinvestment and a return on that capital. Compare the incremental return with its matched opportunity cost and keep the cash-flow timing and terminal assumptions consistent.
</Callout>
