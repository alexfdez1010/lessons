This single graded run covers all six lessons: replication, the binomial tree, Black–Scholes, Greeks, implied volatility and the state-price foundation beneath risk-neutral valuation. For a stated frictionless model, use its given payment dates, state support, rates and borrowing/shorting assumptions. A gross period growth R is a whole-period multiplier. State prices already discount; normalized q weights require one discount. Physical probabilities are not automatically pricing probabilities, and uniqueness requires the relevant payoff to be spanned or additional information.

For European formula questions, use the stated continuous financing/dividend conventions and positive-variance conditions where specified. Exercise probability, spot delta and physical profit probability are different objects. Exactly zero time or volatility has its own payoff rule; theta signs require their rate, dividend and exercise assumptions.
