## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Keep the model and the path separate">
Kelly optimises expected log growth within a specified distribution and set of
constraints. Convert log growth with exp(g) minus one, solve the binary zero
exactly, and label quadratic identities as model-specific. Finite losses and
practical stopping floors remain relevant even when ideal fractional wealth stays positive.
</Callout>
